## Respect the piece named in typed bishop moves

With bishops on c1 and c3 and a knight on b1, `B1d2` is legal SAN for the
rank-one bishop. The case-insensitive coordinate parser instead read `b1d2`
and silently played `Nd2`. The same issue affected Black's `B8d7`, and even
illegal bishop notation could play a legal knight move.

Uppercase `B` with a rank discriminator now follows strict bishop notation.
Explicit coordinate forms `b1d2`, `B1D2` and `B1-d2` retain their meaning.
Rejected moves keep the board unchanged and the typed draft focused for repair.

Three new unit regressions fail before the fix; all six new cases pass afterward.
Production Chromium, Firefox and WebKit verify both colours, three coordinate
forms, rejected moves and correction at desktop, phone and 320px/200% text.
Chromium also passes the existing typed-move, bishop-guidance and Commands-to-move
workflows. `npm run verify` passes with 2,155 tests and the existing skip.
